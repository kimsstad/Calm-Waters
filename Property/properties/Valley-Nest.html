The space

Valley Nest is a welcoming three-bedroom apartment in the charming, Mediterranean-style Beau Rivage Estate in Plettenberg Bay. Set within a secure estate along the Piesang Valley River, this cosy retreat pairs old-Plett charm with the convenience of beaches, restaurants and shops close at hand.

A flight of stairs leads to the private outdoor patio, where a small dining table offers a lovely spot for morning coffee, a leisurely breakfast or a quiet moment in the fresh air.

Inside, the air-conditioned, open-plan dining and lounge area is a comfortable place to gather over a meal or settle in after a day of sunshine and sandy toes. Traditional furnishings give the apartment a warm, homely feel, while a dedicated workspace offers a convenient spot to catch up on work when needed.

All three bedrooms have air conditioning and their own en-suite bathrooms.

Bedroom 1: king bed (en-suite bathroom with bath, overhead shower and double basins)
Bedroom 2: queen bed (en-suite bathroom with bath, overhead shower and double basins)
Bedroom 3: two single beds (en-suite bathroom with shower)

All three bathrooms have heated towel rails, adding a little extra comfort on cooler mornings.

The main bedroom sits beside the lounge, while the second bedroom enjoys a pretty glimpse towards the communal swimming pool, Piesang Valley River and Beacon Isle beyond. The cosy twin bedroom is located just off the kitchen and is particularly well suited to children.

The galley kitchen is fully equipped for easy self-catering, with a stove, oven, large fridge and dishwasher. A washing machine and ironing facilities make longer stays and beach holidays that much more convenient.

Within the estate, the communal swimming pool is a lovely place to spend a slow afternoon, with views across the river towards Beacon Isle Hotel and the beach beyond. Guests can also enjoy the communal squash court, which requires advance booking. Please bring your own racquets and balls, as equipment is not supplied.

Central and Robberg beaches are approximately 600 metres away, making seaside strolls and beach days an easy part of your stay. The nearby Beacon Isle Centre has groceries, a pharmacy and places to eat, including Surf Café and Sotto. A gym is also close by, while Plettenberg Bay Country Club offers golf a short drive away.

Whether you're enjoying a quiet breakfast on the patio, relaxing beside the pool or heading out to explore, Valley Nest offers an easy-going, characterful base for making the most of your time in Plett.

⚠️ Disclaimer: Guests stay at their own risk. The owners are not liable for any injury, accident, loss or damage during the stay. Use of the pool, stairs or communal facilities is at your own discretion.

Where you'll be

Plettenberg Bay

Set along the Piesang Valley River, Beau Rivage is a charming, Mediterranean-style estate that puts the pleasures of a Plett holiday within easy reach. Central and Robberg beaches are just 600 metres away - an easy stroll for days spent soaking up the sunshine and sea air.

The nearby Beacon Isle Centre has everyday necessities covered, with a KWIKSPAR, pharmacy and inviting places to eat, including Surf Café and Sotto. Pop out for a leisurely breakfast, pick up essentials or enjoy dinner close to home.

Within the secure estate, the communal swimming pool offers lovely views across the river towards Beacon Isle Hotel and the beach beyondb - a beautiful spot to unwind between outings. Guests can also enjoy the communal squash court, while a gym is a short distance away and Plettenberg Bay Country Club offers golf nearby.

With beaches, restaurants and everyday conveniences close at hand, Beau Rivage makes a wonderfully easy base for enjoying Plett at your own pace.

Feel free to browse my Guidebook for more recommendations on nearby restaurants, attractions and things to do.

Getting around

Valley Nest’s convenient location makes it easy to enjoy the neighbourhood on foot. Central and Robberg beaches are just 600 metres away, while the nearby Beacon Isle Centre has shops, cafés, restaurants and a pharmacy - perfect for picking up essentials or heading out for a leisurely meal.

For outings further afield, a car is useful for exploring Robberg Nature Reserve, Plett’s surrounding attractions and the scenic Garden Route. Local taxi services are another option; we recommend arranging your trips in advance, particularly during busy holiday periods.
