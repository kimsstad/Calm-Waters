About this space

Solana Apartment is a bright and welcoming ground-floor holiday apartment situated in the secure Goose Valley Golf Estate in Plettenberg Bay. Accommodating up to 4 guests, it offers a relaxed setting with a lovely garden-facing patio and convenient access to the estate's leisure facilities.

The apartment comprises 2 bedrooms and 2 bathrooms. The main bedroom is furnished with a king-size bed and has an en-suite bathroom fitted with a shower and toilet. The second bedroom contains 2 single beds, with a separate bathroom also fitted with a shower and toilet.

The open-plan living area connects the fully equipped kitchen, dining area and comfortable lounge, creating a welcoming space for guests to spend time together. Glass doors lead out onto a private, garden-facing patio with outdoor seating, 2 sun loungers and braai facilities, ideal for enjoying relaxed meals and sunny afternoons.

Guests have exclusive use of the entire apartment and patio, as well as a dedicated parking bay. The estate offers a range of shared facilities, including a communal swimming pool, children's play area, golf course, restaurant, clubhouse, tennis and squash courts, and a walkway leading to the lagoon. Please note that the communal facilities are shared with other residents and guests, and opening times, bookings and applicable charges should be confirmed directly with the relevant facilities.

Where you'll be

Plettenberg Bay

Goose Valley Golf Estate is nestled away from the hustle and bustle of the city center, offering a peaceful and tranquil setting in an immaculate and renown golf course. Despite its serene location, the charming town, known for its excellent restaurants and quaint shops, is only 3.9 km away.

In the surrounding area, you can explore various attractions, including vineyards and nature reserves, with bird sanctuaries, cheetah and monkey parks, elephant encounters, a reptile park, and even a puzzle park.

The nearest beaches are just 5km away by car, with several others only a 4 km drive. For hiking enthusiasts, the majestic Robberg Nature Reserve is a must-visit destination.

Getting around

A vehicle is recommended for getting around, though chauffeur services are also available if needed.

No need on the Goose Valley golf estate itself though, the pool is metres away; the lagoon, golf, tennis and squash courts are within walking distance.
