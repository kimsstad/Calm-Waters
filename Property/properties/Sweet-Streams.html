Where you'll be

Keurboomsrivier, Keurboomstrand

Nestled just 8km from the vibrant heart of Plettenberg Bay, Keurbooms River Lodge offers a serene waterside lifestyle that blends effortless convenience with unspoiled nature. Winding along the quiet beach road, you’ll find secure estates perched on the banks of the Keurbooms River, leading to the expansive Keurbooms Beach. Here, gentle estuarine waters beckon for boating, swimming, and fishing, while the riverbank and adjacent indigenous forest invite leisurely strolls—where you might spot duiker, grysbok or vervet monkeys among the trees—before emerging onto pristine dunes and the surf of the Indian Ocean.

The Keurbooms River Reserve protects this exquisite estuary habitat, making it a bird‑watcher’s paradise filled with Knysna loeries, fish eagles, and the endemic Cape seahorse tucked among eel‑grass beds. When you’re ready for a taste of local life, a handful of quality restaurants, a deli and coffee shop all overlook river or sea vistas just minutes from your door. Whether you’re seeking sunrise paddles through calm lagoon waters, sunset picnics on estate lawns, or a short drive into Plettenberg Bay’s shops, galleries and eateries, the Keurbooms neighborhood delivers a perfect blend of tranquility and easy access to coastal adventure.

Getting around

Getting around Keurbooms River Lodge is straightforward whether you arrive by car or use local transport. Situated just off the N2 highway, the estate lies approximately 6 km (a nine‑minute drive) from the centre of Plettenberg Bay, and guests benefit from ample free, private on‑site parking.

For those who prefer not to drive, taxis in Plettenberg Bay charge around ZAR 80 for a typical 5 km journey, with fares calculated at roughly ZAR 20 base plus ZAR 12 per kilometre. Ride‑share services operate similarly, and drivers can be booked via local apps or by phoning one of the area’s reputable cab companies.

A uniquely scenic option is the Keurbooms River ferry, which departs twice daily from Plettenberg Bay Marina to the river lodge. This 6 km boat ride takes about 30 minutes each way and costs ZAR 260 per person, offering a tranquil introduction to the river’s forested gorge.

If you prefer to stretch your legs, well‑maintained estate pathways lead you through manicured gardens to the riverbank and Keurbooms Beach in under 15 minutes on foot. Whether by car, taxi, ferry or on foot, exploring the immediate neighbourhood and accessing Plettenberg Bay’s shops, restaurants and attractions is both easy and enjoyable.
