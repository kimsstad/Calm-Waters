The space

Clementine Cottage is a delightful self-catering garden cottage set within an established property in Plettenberg Bay’s elevated Cutty Sark area. Filled with natural light and gentle character, its soft white interiors are warmed by terracotta-toned floors, sunny yellow details, fresh green accents, graceful arches and decorative cornices. Cheerful rather than contemporary, the cottage has a wonderfully nostalgic personality that makes it feel instantly welcoming.

The cottage accommodates up to four guests across two bedrooms, each with its own full en-suite bathroom.

Bedroom 1: two single beds placed together and a full en-suite bathroom
Bedroom 2: two single beds placed together and a full en-suite bathroom

Please note that the beds remain two individual single mattresses placed side by side rather than one standard double bed.

Both bathrooms are charming features in their own right, with distinctive green-and-white checkerboard floors and colourful yellow detailing. Each en-suite includes a bathtub, separate shower, toilet, basin and heated towel rail, giving every bedroom the convenience of a private, fully appointed bathroom.

At the heart of the cottage is a bright and comfortable living area where the lounge and dining space flow naturally together. Soft furnishings, floral touches and collected décor give the room a relaxed, lived-in warmth, while the dining table comfortably seats four. French doors open the interior towards the patio and garden, allowing fresh air and sunlight to move through the cottage.

Although compact, the kitchen is thoughtfully stocked for easy self-catering. It includes a stove, bar fridge, crockery, cutlery, knives, chopping boards, bowls, pots, pans and the everyday essentials needed to prepare and enjoy meals at home. While there is no full-sized refrigerator, the bar fridge is suitable for keeping a smaller selection of groceries and drinks during your stay.

Outside, the large covered patio is made for slow holiday mornings and unhurried afternoons. Enjoy breakfast around the outdoor table, settle into a sun lounger with a book, or simply take in the peaceful garden surroundings. Beyond the greenery, beautiful distant views unfold towards the lagoon, bay, ocean and surrounding mountains.

Clementine Cottage is a separate guest unit situated on the same larger property as the main house. It offers a warm and character-filled base from which to enjoy Plettenberg Bay, with comfortable living spaces, two private en-suite bedrooms and an inviting connection to the garden and scenery beyond.

Please note that the fireplace in the lounge is strictly off-limits and may not be used, as lighting it could damage the surrounding flooring.

Whether you are visiting as a small family, two couples or a group of friends, Clementine Cottage offers a cheerful and memorable Plett stay filled with colour, comfort and old-world charm.

Where you’ll be

Plettenberg Bay, Western Cape, South Africa

Cutty Sark is a well-established, elevated residential neighbourhood situated above central Plettenberg Bay. Set along a leafy hillside, the area is known for its sweeping outlooks across the lagoon, bay, ocean, mountains and Robberg Peninsula. It offers a peaceful setting while remaining conveniently close to the heart of town.

Plettenberg Bay’s CBD and Main Street are just 1.3 km away, offering cafés, restaurants, bakeries, boutiques and everyday essentials. The Market Square Shopping Centre is 3.1 km away and has more than 50 stores, including supermarkets, a pharmacy and a selection of speciality shops.

Central Beach is 2.1 km from the cottage, Lookout Beach is 2.3 km away, and the long sandy shoreline of Robberg Beach is 3.4 km away. Plettenberg Bay Country Club is 3.5 km away, while the renowned Robberg Nature Reserve, with its spectacular coastal scenery and hiking trails, is 9 km away.

With its elevated views, peaceful residential atmosphere and convenient access to beaches, shops, restaurants and outdoor attractions, Cutty Sark provides an ideal base for enjoying Plettenberg Bay and the surrounding Garden Route.
