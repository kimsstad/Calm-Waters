The space

Serendipity - Ocean View Apartment is a welcoming first-floor apartment in central Plettenberg Bay, ideally positioned within walking distance of restaurants, shops and the beach. Its elevated position makes the most of the setting, with beautiful views across the town towards the ocean and mountains.

As you step inside, your eye is immediately drawn through the open-plan living area towards the balcony and the views beyond. The apartment combines comfortable coastal living with some more contemporary touches, including vinyl flooring and a modern, fully equipped kitchen.

The apartment accommodates up to four guests across two bedrooms and two bathrooms.

Bedroom 1: double bed (en-suite bathroom with shower)
Bedroom 2: double bed (separate bathroom with bathtub)

The main bedroom has its own en-suite bathroom and a window framing a lovely ocean view. The second bedroom is a smaller, cosy room, with the second bathroom conveniently located right next door and fitted with a bathtub.

The open-plan living space includes a fully equipped kitchen, a dining area for four and a relaxed lounge area with a daybed-style seat and Smart TV. Large windows bring in plenty of natural light and draw the ocean views into the living space.

From here, step out onto the private first-floor balcony - one of the apartment’s best features. With views across Plettenberg Bay towards the ocean and mountains, it is a lovely spot to enjoy a morning coffee, an evening drink or a relaxed braai.

Guests also have access to the complex’s communal swimming pool and leisure area, where you can enjoy a swim or make use of facilities such as table tennis and darts. A communal laundry room is also available for added convenience, and secure parking is provided within the gated complex.

The apartment is in a very central and convenient part of Plett, which means it is situated next to a BUSY ROAD. However, double-glazed windows help to reduce outside noise while still allowing guests to enjoy the benefits of being close to the town, restaurants, shops and beach.

Whether you’re spending your days at the beach, exploring Plett or simply enjoying the view from the balcony, Serendipity - Ocean View Apartment offers a convenient and comfortable base in the heart of town.

No matriculants or “ragers” are permitted.

Where you’ll be

Plettenberg Bay, Western Cape, South Africa

Serendipity - Ocean View Apartment is set in a lively, central part of Plettenberg Bay, surrounded by residential buildings and local amenities. The apartment is situated on a main road, with the popular Market Off Main conveniently located just across the road.

Plettenberg Bay’s Main Street is only around a 5-minute walk away, putting guests within easy reach of shops, cafés, restaurants and everyday essentials. The beach is also within walking distance, making it easy to enjoy both the town and the coastline without needing to drive everywhere.

With restaurants, shopping and the beach all close by, the area is ideal for guests who enjoy being in the heart of Plett and having everything within easy reach.

Feel free to browse my Guidebook for more recommendations on nearby restaurants, attractions and things to do.

Getting around

Serendipity - Ocean View Apartment is conveniently located for exploring central Plettenberg Bay on foot, with shops, restaurants, cafés and the beach all within walking distance. There is on-site parking for one motor vehicle if you’re travelling by car, and local taxi services are available if you need transport around town or to attractions further afield.
