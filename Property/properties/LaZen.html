About this space

Nestled on a hillside above Plettenberg Bay, La-Zen is the luxurious sister apartment of La-Lapa and La-Med, elevated at treetop height with sweeping ocean and sunset views. Tucked away from the bustle of town yet conveniently close to shops and amenities, it offers a peaceful, private setting.

With elegant Asian-inspired touches, this intimate apartment invites couples to slow down, soak up the scenery and enjoy a romantic coastal escape, with the best of Plettenberg Bay within easy reach.

The space

Blending modern, Asian-inspired design with natural textures and a tranquil garden setting, La-Zen offers a peaceful coastal escape overlooking Plettenberg Bay. Warm timber finishes, thoughtful decorative details and inviting outdoor spaces create an atmosphere of comfort, character and relaxation.

The experience begins in a delightful entrance courtyard, where lush greenery, decorative hanging birdcages and a cosy seating area create a welcoming first impression. An outdoor shower adds to the relaxed, coastal atmosphere before stepping inside the studio.

The open-plan interior is thoughtfully arranged for comfort and convenience. A well-equipped kitchenette features an air fryer, electric skillet, microwave, espresso machine and coffee plunger, ideal for preparing light meals and enjoying a leisurely morning coffee. Opposite, the spacious bathroom area features a beautiful freestanding clawfoot bath, walk-in shower, double vanity and storage.

The sleeping area features two single beds positioned side by side beneath a charming canopy, surrounded by soft curtains that create a cosy, intimate retreat. An additional curtain between the beds provides optional privacy for travelling companions. A comfortable little TV corner and fold-out dining table for two complete the living space.

Beyond the studio, outdoor living takes centre stage. A beautiful wooden patio offers several inviting places to unwind, from a sheltered daybed to wooden swings suspended beneath the pergola. A small ornamental pond nestled within a pebble courtyard, surrounded by lush planting and natural stone, brings a peaceful, Zen-inspired touch to the space. Alongside the apartment, a private garden with natural stone, lush greenery and decorative sculptures offers another tranquil retreat. Additional seating and outdoor dining areas invite guests to slow down and enjoy the garden surroundings, with sweeping views across Plettenberg Bay, the ocean and distant mountains.

The gardens extend towards a shared lawn, while the apartment's private outdoor spaces remain clearly defined. With its own entrance and secure parking, the apartment offers a sense of privacy while forming part of a property with two other independent units.

Designed to accommodate two adults, this studio is ideal for couples or travelling companions seeking a charming, nature-filled retreat close to Plettenberg Bay's amenities.

⚠️ Disclaimer: Guests stay at their own risk. The owners are not liable for any injury, accident, loss, or damage during the stay. Use of all available facilities is at your own discretion.

Potential for noise: The apartment is situated on top of a hill with the N2 passing below.

Guest access

The apartment is entirely private for your exclusive use during your stay, including its own entrance, courtyard, patio and garden. While the property hosts a two other independent apartments, all guest areas are fully separate. The only shared spaces are the front yard and secure on-site parking. Guests are welcome to relax, unwind, and enjoy the views and outdoor spaces at their own pace.

Other things to note

What sets this apartment apart is its elevated hillside position, offering expansive ocean and sunset views with a sense of privacy and natural surroundings, while still being conveniently close to Plettenberg Bay’s shops, restaurants, and beaches.

Please note that South Africa experiences scheduled electricity interruptions. The apartment is equipped with an inverter that powers essential items such as lights, TV, Wi-Fi, laptops, and mobile phones. In rare cases of severe outages, connectivity may still be affected. We appreciate your understanding.

The property is situated on a hill above the N2 highway, and occasional traffic noise may be audible during your stay.

Where you'll be

Plettenberg Bay, Western Cape, South Africa

La-Zen is perched on a hillside above Plettenberg Bay, offering sweeping ocean, bay, and sunset views from an elevated and tranquil setting. The surrounding natural landscape creates a sense of seclusion and calm, with elevated vantage points that minimise nearby activity.

While the setting feels peaceful and removed from the energy of town life, Plettenberg Bay's main shopping areas, restaurants, and beaches are all close by, offering the perfect balance between relaxation and convenience.

The property is located close to a nearby settlement and sits above the N2 highway, meaning occasional traffic noise may be heard. The property remains secure and is managed to ensure guests' comfort and peace of mind.

Getting around

A car is recommended for most sightseeing and excursions in and around Plettenberg Bay, particularly when exploring beaches, nature reserves, and the wider Garden Route.

Taxi services and shuttle options are available in Plett, providing added convenience for guests who prefer not to drive during their stay.
